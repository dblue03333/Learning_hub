# Reading Notes: Machine learning in geriatric care: a scoping review of models using multidimensional assessment data

> Tip: Use the 3-pass approach — Pass 1 (10 min): Title, Abstract, Intro, Headings, Conclusion. Pass 2: Figures, Method, Results. Pass 3: Details & Reproduction.

## 1. Goal
This scoping review maps ML research in geriatric care and provides an overview of the field.

## 2. Key Findings (from 40 Studies)
- **Common predicted outcomes:** Falls, frailty, functional decline, mortality, and hospital readmission.
- **Validation gap:** Studies only used internal validation; none performed external validation.
- **Top model:** XGBoost achieved the best performance most often, but most studies did not report model calibration.

## 3. Problems & Research Gaps
- **Limited generalizability:** Most studies were conducted in high-income countries, which limits their use in low-resource settings.
- **No external validation:** Models were not tested on independent, outside patient groups.
- **Data issues:** Data quality and missing-data handling were often weak.

## 4. Clinical Takeaway (Why This Matters in Real Hospitals)
- Different hospitals use different assessment tools and data formats, making it difficult to transfer a model from Hospital A to Hospital B.
